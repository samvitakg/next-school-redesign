About us

Engaging, rewardingand relevant

NEXT school is committed to making learning more engaging, rewarding and relevant. We are India’s first Big Picture Learning school. NEXT is supported by Marathon Group, a real estate developer in Mumbai, through its charitable enterprise – Ramniklal Zaverbhai Shah Trust.

Learn about Big Picture
Our mission

Discover passions, unleash the drive to learn

NEXT School aims to help students discover their passions and interests and unleash their innate drive to learn and contribute to the world. We personalise each student’s learning and, along with fulfilling academic requirements, help them do authentic, real world projects based on their interests where they learn by doing. Through this approach, we hope to help students learn how to do something productive and meaningful in the real world by doing what they love, which we believe will lead to a fulfilling life. Our approach encourages students to understand and appreciate differences and become compassionate, lifelong learners.

Our team

A passionate group of educators

We are a passionate group of educators determined to bring about a much needed upgrade to the current standardised system of schooling. Our team has extensive experience with leading international schools across the city and has recently undergone rigorous training in the US and in Australia by world-renowned thinkers and practitioners. We are united by a common goal – to bring the highest quality educational experience to our children.

Parmeet Shah

CEO, Big Picture India and NEXT

Parmeet is the founder and CEO of Next and also works at Marathon Group. He has been scouring the globe for the best educational models for the last 6 years. He holds a BA in Economics from Yale University and an MS from Columbia University, USA.

Elliot Washor

Co-Founder, Big Picture Learning

Elliot, who is the co-founder of Big Picture Learning is our coach, mentor and guide. He has been involved in school reform for more than 30 years as a teacher, principal, administrator and writer. He leads Big Picture’s international endeavours and has been working very closely with our team.

Radhika Dharmarajan

Head of School

Radhika has over 25 years of teaching and leadership experience having taught in Mumbai and also overseas in Indonesia. She has founded 2 preschools and an inclusive school in India. She holds an International Montessori diploma, a B.Ed and M.Ed.

Kavita Anantharaman

Primary Years Programme (PYP) Coordinator

Over the course of a decade, Kavita has amassed considerable experience as an IB PYP educator. Her proficiency lies in crafting engaging learning environments, designing curriculum and implementing Big Picture strategies. Equipped with a Masters in Computer Applications, she brings a unique blend of expertise to her role.

Nikhil Bangera

Middle Years Programme (MYP) Coordinator

Nikhil has spent the last 10 years setting up and leading programs at NEXT, Mumbai. Viren Parmar

Diploma Programme (IBDP) Coordinator

Viren holds a unique combination of a degree in Physics and a Postgraduate Diploma in Counseling. With over seven years of teaching and learning experience, Viren is deeply committed to understanding human behavior and emotional well-being.

Our trustees

The trust behind NEXT

R.Z. Shah

Trustee

RZ Shah is the founding father of Marathon. Many may know that before he started the business in 1969, he was actually an educationist. He’s had a very deep association with the very first school in Mulund – SMPR. He started as a student, became a teacher, and then the principal, then also became a trustee and president of the trust. For more than 50 years he has had a close relationship with this school, so education runs very deep in his veins. It has been a long standing dream of his to start a school in his hometown, for which he has spared no expense.

Mayur Shah

Trustee

Mr. Mayur Shah, Managing Director of Marathon Group has over 25 years of rich and varied experience in the real estate and construction industry. He is a civil engineer from the University of Bombay and MS in structural engineering from University of Oklahoma, USA. He is currently the President-Elect of MCHI, the association of real estate developers in Maharashtra and also the Vice President of Jain Jagruti Group.
