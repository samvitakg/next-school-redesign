Admissions process

01

Visit us

Schedule a 1-on-1 session with a member from our team to know more about our unique programs. You will be assigned a single point of contact from our team.

02

Getting to know your child

After you express interest to move further with the admission process, we would like to meet the child and get to know them better.

03

04

Submit your application

You can then submit the required information along with the documents on a digital admission form shared with you by our team. The admission will be confirmed upon receipt of the admissions deposit.
