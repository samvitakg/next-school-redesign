Big Picture is a revolutionary educational framework that has received widespread acclaim from world leaders and experts alike. It puts students at the centre of their own learning by personalising each child’s education. It focuses on real world learning and helps children develop 21st century skills like creativity, collaboration, communication, planning and goal setting. It’s considered by many to be the best educational system in the world.

School design

Welcome to NEXT, Lower Parel!

The school is located at Marathon Futurex, Lower Parel. Next has a dedicated entrance on ground level so pick up and drop off becomes convenient. Next Lower Parel is situated at Marathon Futurex – a premium commercial space in Lower Parel which has offices for some of the biggest brands in India.

n

The advisory

The advisory is the heart of a Big Picture School. This is a learner’s home room where they learn, play and collaborate together. The size and style of furniture changes for learners of different age groups.

≡

Art & design

We have a dedicated space for art and design. Learners can find art supplies and makerspace gear here to create amazing things!

x

Seriously playful!

We take play very seriously at Next. So we try and make every space as playful and exciting for learners as we can. Even a passage doesn’t stay a passage!

+

Let’s get loud!

Introducing Loud Commons – the playful community space that ties together all the other spaces. Lunch, meetings, play-time – it all happens here!

Meeting spaces

We have a dedicated meeting room for learners. We also have lots of tables and seating in the community spaces for informal meetings.

Sky Garden

NEXT Steps · Montessori

Born to explore

Little children come into this world burning to explore, but we sit them down in boring classrooms that kill this drive and curiosity. Our Montessori program gives tremendous autonomy and freedom even to the littlest children, to keep this fire alive – to enable them to be self-directed, independent and fully engaged.

Our team

NEXT, Lower Parel team

Parmeet Shah

CEO, Big Picture India and NEXT

Parmeet is the founder and CEO of Next and also works at Marathon Group. He has been scouring the globe for the best educational models for the last 6 years. He holds a BA in Economics from Yale University and an MS from Columbia University, USA.

Elliot Washor

Co-Founder, Big Picture Learning

Elliot, who is the co-founder of Big Picture Learning is our coach, mentor and guide. He has been involved in school reform for more than 30 years as a teacher, principal, administrator and writer. He leads Big Picture’s international endeavours and has been working very closely with our team.

Radhika Dharmarajan

Program Director

Radhika has over 25 years of teaching and leadership experience having taught in Mumbai and also overseas in Indonesia. She has founded 2 preschools and an inclusive school in India. She holds an International Montessori diploma, a B.Ed and M.Ed.

Priya Ramesh

Program Manager

Priya has been part of NEXT school for over six years. She leads Career Counselling and Learning through Internship (LTI), works closely with learners on internships, university strategy, and applications. Her experience spans education, capacity building and organisational development, with a strong research foundation. She holds a Master’s degree in Development from Azim Premji University.

Seema Jain

Program Manager

Seema has been working for 20 plus years as an educator. She has taught in India, Singapore, UK along with in-service training and teaching experiences in Japan. Seema has been associated with Next school for the past 9 years and leads the Next Plus program at Next. She has a Diploma from National Institute of Education (NIE) Singapore and has a B.Ed and M.Ed from SNDT women’s university, Juhu.
