Curriculum at NEXT · Mulund

An IB WorldSchool

We are an IB World School offering a continuum of all four programmes – the Primary Years Programme (PYP), Middle Years Programme (MYP), Diploma Programme (DP) and Career-related Programme (CP).

MulundProgramsIB curriculumSchool designAdmissions
The IB continuum

All four programmes

The institutions that offer at least three of the core programmes are referred to as continuum schools.

PYP

Primary Years Programme

Nursery to Grade 5

Workshops address the academic requirements like literacy, maths and other subject specific areas. These are done through inquiry driven, trans-disciplinary themes as prescribed by the IB PYP program.

MYP

Middle Years Programme

Grade 6 to 10

With the MYP program, we move to a context and concept based approach which brings together different academic disciplines. It helps students learn about different phenomenon, solve problems and create products.

DP

Diploma Programme

Grade 11 and 12

At the grade 11 and 12 level, students can choose to take up the IB Diploma Program, which has higher academic expectations.

CP

Career-related Programme

Grade 11 and 12

The IB Career Program is more focused on real world work and careers. Subjects currently offered under the Career-related Programme (CP) are Business studies and Artificial Intelligence.

Why have we chosen IB?

The IB programmes

01

Independent thinkers

Encourage students to think independently and drive their own learning.

02

The best universities

Enable students to take part in programmes of education that can lead them to the best universities in India and overseas.

03

The whole child

Address not only cognitive development but social, emotional and physical wellbeing.

04

A globalised world

Prepare students to engage with people in an increasingly globalized, rapidly changing world.

How do IB and Big Picture work together?

Complementary by design

Big Picture and the International Baccalaureate complement each other incredibly well due to their belief in an education for the 21st century. Both encourage students to drive their own learning.

Big Picture: the how

Big Picture focuses on the how – how do we structure and personalise learning for each child, how do we develop their interests and passions and how do we connect them with the real world.

IB: the requirements

The IB framework helps us ensure that students are meeting academic requirements, learning about different subjects and building age appropriate skills.
