FAQs

NEXT Hub questions

Register now ↗
What is the After School Program about?+
Our program offers a unique blend of curated activities and free play, giving children a balanced environment to learn, create, explore, and unwind after school hours.
Who can join the program?+
The program is open to learners from age 18 months up to Grade 12. Activities are designed to be age-appropriate and guided by trained facilitators.
What kind of activities are included?+
We offer a wide range of activities such as:
Sports & Fitness
Creative Arts
Performing Arts
Cognitive Development
Daily Free Play Time
What is free play and why is it important? Can the Free Play time be skipped?+
Free play allows learners to explore activities independently, socialize, and unwind in a safe space. It supports creativity, decision-making, confidence, and emotional well-being.
Free Play time is part of the program and can not be skipped.
Are the sessions supervised?+
Absolutely. All activities and free play zones are supervised by trained educators and facilitators who ensure safety and engagement.
Do children need to bring any equipment?+
Most materials are provided. For specific activities like sports or arts, we will inform you in advance if anything needs to be brought from home.
Is transportation provided?+
No, Parents will need to make arrangements for the learners to be picked up from school.
What is the fee structure?+
Fee details are available on the NEXT HUB webpage.
How do I register my child?+
You can register through our online form, via the NEXT HUB webpage, or by contacting the school office directly.
Will children be given snacks?+
No, parents will need to send an extra snack with the learners.
Can children join specific activities only?+
Learners can choose up to 1 Activity per day and up to 5 for the whole week.
Who runs the program?+
The program is facilitated by experienced educators, activity specialists, and trained coaches who ensure high-quality learning experiences.
Will we get a makeover session if the learner is absent on a particular day?+
There will not be a makeover session for learners being absent on a particular day. However, if the school decides to cancel any session for unavoidable situations, a makeover session will be provided on a later date.
Can I request a refund or Transfer from an activity if my kid does not enjoy?+
Fees once paid are non-refundable and non-transferable. However, in exceptional circumstances, the school reserves the right to review and consider requests as deemed appropriate.
Will the activities be conducted on school and public holidays?+
The after-school activities will be conducted only on school working days and not during vacations and public holidays. A detailed calendar shall be shared with all parents after registrations.
How will registrations work?+
The seats are booked on first cum first serve basis.
What are the timings of the program?+
The program runs from 3:45 pm to 5:30 pm Monday to Friday and a few activities on Weekends. Learners will get Free Play time from 3:45 pm to 4:30 and Learning Time from 4:30 pm to 5:30 pm.
