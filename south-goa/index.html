Welcome to the revolution

A radically different approach

The traditional education system is broken – children spend 20,000+ hours doing what they are told and learning content that they forget soon after the exam.

Next School brings a radically different approach to education, where instead of focusing on irrelevant academics, students take on real world projects and internships based on their own interests.

▶ Film · 5 min Next School in 5 minutes

Big Picture is a revolutionary educational framework that has received widespread acclaim from world leaders and experts alike. It puts students at the centre of their own learning by personalising each child’s education. It focuses on real world learning and helps children develop 21st century skills like creativity, collaboration, communication, planning and goal setting. It’s considered by many to be the best educational system in the world.

Montessori · South Goa

Born to explore

Children come into this world burning to explore, but we sit them down in boring classrooms that kill this drive and curiosity. Our Montessori program gives tremendous autonomy and freedom even to the littlest children, to keep this fire alive – to enable them to be self-directed, independent and fully engaged.

Our team

NEXT, South Goa team

Parmeet Shah

CEO, Big Picture India and NEXT

Parmeet is the founder and CEO of Next and also works at Marathon Group. He has been scouring the globe for the best educational models for the last 6 years. He holds a BA in Economics from Yale University and an MS from Columbia University, USA.

Elliot Washor

Co-Founder, Big Picture Learning

Elliot, who is the co-founder of Big Picture Learning is our coach, mentor and guide. He has been involved in school reform for more than 30 years as a teacher, principal, administrator and writer. He leads Big Picture’s international endeavours and has been working very closely with our team.

Sukriti Pant

Advisor

Sukriti has spent the last six years supporting language development through play and daily routines at Fröbel Kindergarten in Potsdam, Germany. Prior to this, she led a learning centre for first-generation learners with H.E.L.G.O.e.V. in Howrah. She holds an M.A. in Education from Azim Premji University and is pursuing an M.A. in Childhood Studies and Children’s Rights at Fachhochschule Potsdam.

Nikhil Bangera

Program Director

Nikhil has spent the last 10 years setting up and leading programs at NEXT, Mumbai. Prior to NEXT, he spent a decade developing educational content and curricula for students and adults at Packt Publishing, Zaya Learning Labs, and Kotak Education Foundation. He holds an M.A. in Education from Azim Premji University, an M.A. in Sociology, and a B.E. in Computer Science from Mumbai University.

Amolika Lavate

Advisor

Amolika has been a member of NEXT, Mumbai for the past five years and is an integral part of the Early Years Programme. With over 17 years of experience, she brings a strong understanding of early childhood education. She is deeply passionate about the Montessori approach and a holistic, big-picture view of learning. She has worked with institutions in Mumbai, served as an Academic Coordinator in Bengaluru, and has been a Montessori trainer at RKC. She holds a B.E. in Computer Science from Pune University.

Poonam Augustine

Maisy Pereira
