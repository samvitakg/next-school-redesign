We are open for admissions for the academic year 2026-27.

Limited seats available. Admissions are taken on first come first serve basis.

Admissions process

01

Consultation call

Schedule a call with a member from our team to know more about our unique program. You will be assigned a single point of contact from our team.

02

Visit the campus

After you express interest to move further with the admission process, we would meet you and your child at our campus.

03

Admission decision

Post our interaction, we’ll confirm our decision on the admission within a week.

04

Submit your documents

You submit the required documents on a digital admission form. The admission will be confirmed upon receipt of the Admission Fees.

Other admissions information

  • The fee you sign up on will remain the base throughout the child’s time in our program and will see inflationary increases between 8% to 10% per year compounded. Please note that this does not guarantee admission and will be subject to successful completion of the admissions process.
  • Withdrawal or cancellation – if the admission is withdrawn at any point after paying, the Admission Fee will be forfeited and only the tuition paid will be returned.
  • The above fees excludes charges for meals, transportation, and any after program activities. These will be conveyed to you at the time of admission or as applicable.
  • Tuition fees can be paid in quarterly instalments. Tuition for NEXT Steps (2-3 year age group) has to be paid in half-yearly instalments.
