Born to explore

Children come into this world burning to explore, but we sit them down in boring classrooms that kill this drive and curiosity. Our Montessori program gives tremendous autonomy and freedom even to the littlest children, to keep this fire alive – to enable them to be self-directed, independent and fully engaged.

▶ Podcast · 45 min The Montessori approach: deep dive

Watch our 45 minute video podcast where our founder, head of school and program leader discuss how Montessori approach is completely different from the traditional approach.

Key features

01

Autonomous learning

Montessori is a revolutionary program in the sense that it’s the only program that allows for a very high degree of autonomy and freedom in a mixed age group setting!

02

Developing independence

From a very young age, children are making their own decisions about what to do, when to do it and also developing an ability to do things by themselves.

03

Personalisation

Montessori education values the unique abilities of each child, allowing them to advance at their own pace without unnecessary competition, fostering intrinsic motivation and self-discipline.

04

Holistic learning

Montessori education nurtures the development of the whole child—intellectual, social, emotional, and physical—with hands-on experiences.

Key skills

Self-care

Washing, dressing, toileting, and eating, according to each child’s individual capacity.

Care of the environment

Cleaning, food preparation and food service; plant care and animal care.

Large-motor activities

Walking, climbing, running, jumping, balancing, climbing steps, and more.

Fine-motor skills

Reaching, grasping, picking up objects, transferring objects, using tools and utensils, doing art work.

Language

Naming objects, describing actions and intentions, discussing pictures, conversation, music, and singing.

Social skills

Developing skills through interactions with peers, advisors.
